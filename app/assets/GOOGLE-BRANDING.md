# Google sign-in artwork

`google-g.png` is the unmodified Google G asset downloaded from:
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png

Source and brand usage guidelines, checked 2026-09-09:
https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines

The Google mark identifies the Google authorization flow. Google retains its brand rights; the asset is not UFriend's own logo. It is bundled locally so rendering the button does not contact Google before the user chooses to connect.
